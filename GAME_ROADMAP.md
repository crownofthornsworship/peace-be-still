# Peace, Be Still 2.0 — Implementation Status

## Product goal

The repeated scavenger-hunt prototype has been replaced by ten authored sailing voyages with true heading/throttle controls, navigation, service, delivery, wildlife discovery, reefs, rescue, fog, storm preparation, and a multi-stage Mark 4:35–41 finale.

## Implemented — Sailing feel

- Improve acceleration, turning, braking, collision response, wake, and camera follow.
- Keep objectives and touch controls readable in both portrait and landscape.
- Add clear voyage goals, discovery tracking, checkpoints, and a gentle recovery when the boat gets stuck.
- Separate decorative creatures from hazards so players can read the lake safely.

## Implemented — Ten authored voyages

- Introduce navigation, currents, narrow passages, fog, wind, waves, and rescue objectives gradually.
- Give each voyage one main mechanic, optional discoveries, and a recognizable landmark.
- Add quiet interludes for story, reflection, and creature encounters between harder voyages.
- Make later difficulty come from navigation decisions, not excessive speed.

## Implemented — World and animation foundation

- Upgrade boat animation, passengers, sail/rope motion, water interaction, weather transitions, and creature behavior.
- Create dawn, midday, sunset, night, calm, rain, and storm lighting states.
- Add layered shorelines, distant settlements, birds, fish, turtles, and environmental sound.

## Implemented — Storm finale

- Build a multi-stage final voyage: warning weather, navigation under pressure, peak storm, “Peace, be still,” then restored calm.
- Use cinematic framing without taking control away for too long.
- Add an epilogue, voyage replay, discovery journal, and full-map completion reward.

## Release gate

- Complete all twelve voyages on phone portrait/landscape, tablet, and desktop.
- Verify controls, collisions, checkpoints, reduced motion, sound, saves, reset, and story readability.
- Refresh social preview, metadata, README, Light & Life Games, and Salt & Light Web Studio.
